# EtDox – LinkedIn Lead Generation & Email Automation

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on streamlining the lead generation and personalized email outreach process for EtDox using LinkedIn research, Google Sheets, Zapier, and Gmail.

The objective is to reduce manual work, organize potential business leads, generate personalized outreach messages, and maintain structured lead records.

## 2. Phase 1 – Lead Collection & Personalized Message Generation

### Step 1: Lead Research
- Identify potential business leads through LinkedIn research.
- Collect relevant professional information.
- Focus on business-related and publicly available information.
- Qualify leads based on their professional role and company.

### Step 2: Google Sheets Integration
Store the collected lead information in Google Sheets.

The database structure includes:
- Lead Name
- Designation
- Company Name
- Industry
- LinkedIn Profile URL
- Company Website
- Business Email
- Personalized Message
- Email Status
- Follow-up Information

### Step 3: Automation Using Zapier
Zapier connects the lead database with the message-generation and email workflow.

The automation processes lead information and prepares personalized outreach content using available lead details.

### Step 4: Personalized Message Generation
Generate customized outreach messages using relevant lead information, such as:
- Lead name
- Company name
- Professional designation
- Business context

The objective is to make each outreach message relevant to the recipient rather than using a generic template.

### Step 5: Google Sheets Update
Generated personalized messages are stored back in the corresponding Google Sheets records.

This keeps lead information and generated outreach content organized in one place.

## 3. Technologies Used

- LinkedIn – Lead research
- Google Sheets – Lead database and tracking
- Zapier – Workflow automation
- JavaScript – Data processing where required
- Gmail – Email integration and outreach

## 4. Workflow Benefits

- Reduces repetitive manual work.
- Organizes lead information systematically.
- Supports personalized business outreach.
- Keeps generated messages connected to lead records.
- Provides a foundation for automated follow-up workflows.

## 5. Next Development Phase

Phase 2 and subsequent development focus on Gmail integration, email draft generation, follow-up scheduling, and lead status tracking.

## 6. Security & Privacy

This repository is intended for workflow documentation and sanitized automation code.

No private lead database, personal email addresses, passwords, API keys, access tokens, or confidential business information should be published.
